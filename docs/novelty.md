# SmartBio scientific contribution

Smartphone illumination, color calibration and optical biomarker estimation
already exist in the literature. SmartBio does not claim those components
individually as inventions.

The proposed research contribution is the **integrated experimental framework**:

- quality-aware acquisition and rejection;
- adaptive calibration;
- flash/no-flash ambient-light subtraction;
- multi-device validation;
- unseen-device testing;
- uncertainty-aware rejection;
- separate Hb and bilirubin branches;
- quantitative robustness analysis.

Primary research question:

Can a quality-aware and adaptive optical calibration framework improve the
robustness of smartphone-based non-invasive hemoglobin and bilirubin estimation
across different devices and acquisition conditions?


## Biophysical constraint
Smartphone RGB is treated as a low-dimensional, device-dependent optical measurement. Claims of chromophore concentration require controlled acquisition, external calibration and laboratory reference validation; no absolute spectroscopy claim is made.
